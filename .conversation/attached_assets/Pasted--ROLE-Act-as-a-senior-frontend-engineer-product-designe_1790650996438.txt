Act as a senior frontend engineer, product designer, and e-commerce UX specialist.

Build a **premium frontend-only online fashion store demo** for a Ghanaian thrift clothing business selling cargo pants, joggers, jeans, khakis and other casual streetwear.

This is a **sales demo**, not a production backend application.

Do NOT build authentication, database, admin dashboard, backend APIs, payment processing, or server-side functionality.

# DESIGN DIRECTION

Make the website feel like a modern fashion brand.

Visual references:

* Minimal fashion e-commerce
* Editorial streetwear websites
* Modern Shopify stores
* Apple-level spacing and cleanliness
* Strong typography
* Large imagery
* Subtle animations

Avoid:

* Generic Bootstrap appearance
* Excessive gradients
* Cheap-looking cards
* Excessive rounded rectangles
* Too much text
* Cluttered navigation
* Stock-photo-heavy layouts
* Fake reviews
* Fake customer counts
* Fake discounts
* Fake scarcity timers

Use a refined neutral palette:

* Off-white / cream background
* Black / near-black typography
* Muted gray
* Very subtle accent color

Typography should feel bold and fashionable.

Use large headings and generous whitespace.

# MOST IMPORTANT UX PRINCIPLE

The website is not trying to replace WhatsApp.

It is a **visual storefront that makes WhatsApp ordering easier.**

The ideal customer journey is:

**See product → Like product → Check details → Tap WhatsApp → Confirm availability → Buy**

Make that journey obvious throughout the entire website.
